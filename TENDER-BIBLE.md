## 5. Preference rules (Week 3)

- Systems: **80/20** at or below **R50 million**; **90/10** above. *(Source: PPR 2022 — verify date: ____)*
- Specific goals are set by the **procuring institution** in the tender.
- Preference points must be **claimed and substantiated**.
- Pre-qualification is a **gate**, not points.
