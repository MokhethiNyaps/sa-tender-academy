# Week 0 — Orientation (Day 0, ~4 hours)

Complete this before Week 1. It costs you half a day and saves you weeks.

## 1. Why this matters

Most people who try to enter tender writing fail for administrative reasons, not intellectual ones: no filing system, no source discipline, no record of what they have learned, and an AI habit of generating confident fiction. You are fixing all four today.

## 3. Core concepts

### 3.1 Enacted vs commenced
An Act can be signed and published and still have **no legal force** until a commencement date is proclaimed. The Public Procurement Act 28 of 2024 is in exactly this state. Draft regulations under it are *drafts* — useful for anticipating direction, worthless as authority.

**Practical consequence:** for the next 90 days your authority stack is, in order:
1. The tender document itself (including corrigenda and clarification notes)
2. PFMA or MFMA, depending on the organ of state
3. PPPFA + Preferential Procurement Regulations 2022
4. CIDB prescripts (for construction)
5. Sector legislation (COIDA, NHBRC, OHSA, PSIRA, etc.)
6. Treasury instruction notes and practice notes

### 3.2 National/provincial vs municipal
- **National & provincial** organs of state → PFMA → **SBD** forms.
- **Municipalities & municipal entities** → MFMA → **MBD** forms.

This single distinction predicts which forms appear in a pack. Learn to identify the buyer type in the first 60 seconds.

### 3.3 The source-reference habit
Every statement you ever make about a tender carries a reference: *"Mandatory — Volume 1, clause 4.2.3, page 17."* If you cannot cite it, you do not yet know it. This habit is the entire difference between a specialist and a guesser.

## 4. Setup tasks

1. **Accounts:** register on eTenders (free) and bookmark the opportunity search with filters saved for your provinces.
2. **Folder structure** — create locally and mirror in this workspace:
   ```
   tenders/
     <BuyerAcronym>-<TenderNo>-<ShortTitle>/
       00-source-pack/      (original PDFs, unmodified, read-only)
       01-corrigenda/
       02-analysis/         (matrix, eligibility check, go-no-go)
       03-drafts/           (methodology, CVs, experience sheets)
       04-client-inputs/
       05-final-submission/
       notes.md
   ```
   **Rule:** files in `00-source-pack` are never edited. Ever.
3. **Tender log:** open `worklog/tender-log.csv` and keep it updated after every pack.
4. **Tender Bible:** open `TENDER-BIBLE.md`. Add at least one entry every single study day.
5. **AI workspace:** create a project named *SA Tender Academy*, paste the five ground rules as permanent instructions, and create the seven working chats.

---

## 5. Practical assignment (Day 0)

- Download **one** real tender pack of any kind from eTenders. Complete, including annexures.
- Skim it once for 20 minutes only. Write half a page answering just two questions:
  - What would make this bidder lose before anyone reads their proposal?
  - What would make them score badly even if fully compliant?
- Do not research. Do not use AI. This is a baseline snapshot of your current ability — you will repeat this exact exercise in Week 12 and compare.

Save as `worklog/baseline-day0.md`.
