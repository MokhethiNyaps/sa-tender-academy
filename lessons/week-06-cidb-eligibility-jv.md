## 3. Core concepts

### 3.1 How CIDB eligibility actually works

Given: *"Tenderers must have a CIDB contractor grading designation of 5CE or higher."*

CIDB's guidance is that public clients **specify** the grading requirement, should **only consider eligible contractors**, and must **verify** the contractor's status on the Register. So the buyer will check — your check must match theirs.

**Source:** https://www.cidb.org.za/clients/procurement-guides/applying-register-of-contractors/

### 3.2 The eligibility checklist

| # | Check | Where | Fail consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Class of works matches the required class | Register record | Ineligible |
| 2 | Grade ≥ required grade | Register record | Ineligible |
| 3 | Registration **active** (not lapsed/suspended/expired) at closing | Register record | Ineligible |
| 4 | Registration still valid at **award/verification** | Renewal date | Award risk |
| 5 | CRS/registration number matches the entity bidding (exact legal entity) | CIPC + Register | Ineligible |
| 6 | Tender value within the grade's value limit | Advertisement + guide | Ineligible |
| 7 | Any specialist class separately required | Scope/advertisement | Ineligible |
| 8 | Special conditions for lower grades (if the tender allows them) | Tender conditions | Conditional |
| 9 | Not on a restricted/defaulters register | CSD + Treasury registers | Ineligible |
| 10 | JV arrangement (if used) satisfies the tender's JV rules | Tender + CIDB JV rules | Ineligible |

### 3.3 Concepts to master

- **Grades** — financial capability bands tied to tender-value limits.
- **Classes of works** — the discipline; a contractor may hold several, each with its own grade.
- **CRS numbers** — the contractor's registration identifier; must correspond to the exact legal entity submitting.
- **Active vs suspended** — registration must be current; renewals lapse silently and contractors often do not notice.
- **Upgrades** — a contractor can apply to upgrade; assess realistically whether it can complete before closing. Never plan a bid around a hoped-for upgrade.
- **Potentially emerging (PE) status** — a development mechanism allowing certain contractors to be considered for work above their grade under specified conditions; read the CIDB guide's current rules rather than relying on hearsay.
- **Joint ventures** — combining partners' capability to meet a required designation, subject to CIDB's rules.
- **Tender-value limits** — the ceiling per grade; cross-check against the tender's estimated value including VAT treatment as the tender defines it.
- **Track record requirements** — evidence of completed works of a comparable nature/value, often required for grading and separately for functionality scoring.

### 3.4 Joint ventures

CIDB provides a **Joint Venture Calculator** as part of its construction-register services: https://jointventurecalculator.cidb.org.za/

What you must be able to explain to a contractor:
- A JV's combined designation is **calculated**, not simply the highest partner's grade.
- The **class** requirement still has to be satisfied by the appropriate partner(s).
- Tenders often impose their own JV conditions: signed JV agreement, lead partner, joint and several liability, percentage split, separate compliance documents **per partner** (CSD, tax, B-BBEE, LoGS).
- B-BBEE and preference treatment of a JV is governed by the applicable rules and the tender — verify, never assume.
- A JV doubles the administrative failure surface. Budget for it.

**Drill:** run five realistic partner combinations through the calculator and record the outputs and your interpretation.

### 3.5 The written eligibility determination

Your deliverable format (client-facing):

```
ELIGIBILITY DETERMINATION
Tender:            <no> — <title>          Closing: <date/time>
Required:          <designation> (source: advertisement / clause ref)
Client entity:     <legal name, reg no>
Register status:   <grade/class, active?, verified on <date>, source>
Value limit check: tender value <R> vs grade limit <R>
VERDICT:           ELIGIBLE / NOT ELIGIBLE / ELIGIBLE SUBJECT TO <condition>
Reasoning:         <numbered, each with a source>
Options if not eligible: <JV / do not bid / upgrade feasibility>
Unverified items:  <list — marked MISSING — CLIENT TO PROVIDE>
Prepared by / date:
```

Sign nothing you did not verify. "Client told me" is recorded as such.
