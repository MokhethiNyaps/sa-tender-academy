# Week 12 — Simulated Live Bids (Capstone)

> **Assignment volume:** complete **3 full tender-response simulations**.
> **Running total target:** 60+ tenders analysed, of which **at least 10 construction**.

---

## 1. Why this week matters

Everything so far was practised in pieces. This week you run the whole pipeline end-to-end under time pressure, three times, without help. This is the week that proves you are employable/sellable.

---

## 2. Learning objectives

By Friday you can:
- Execute the complete pipeline unaided: **find → qualify → extract → organise → draft → check → package**.
- Hit the 60–90 minute analysis standard on an unseen pack.
- Produce a submission-ready pack with only client-owned inputs missing.

---

## 3. Simulation rules

1. Choose three **real, live or recently closed** tenders:
   - Simulation 1: professional/general services (RFP or RFB)
   - Simulation 2: general building construction (e.g. 3GB–5GB scale)
   - Simulation 3: civil engineering construction (e.g. 4CE–6CE scale)
2. Use a **real or realistic contractor profile**. You may use a consenting contractor; otherwise build a fixed fictional profile file and treat it as immovable fact — never expand it to suit a requirement.
3. **Timebox** the analysis phase to 90 minutes and log the actual time.
4. Every unknown is `MISSING — CLIENT TO PROVIDE`. Count them at the end — a low count with no invention is the goal.
5. No AI drafting of substance until your own points map exists. AI may format, check and challenge.
6. Red-team each simulation on a **different day** from drafting.

---

## 4. The pipeline — run it in this order every time

### Stage 1 — Intake (15 min)
Use `templates/tender-intake-checklist.md`. Record: tender number, buyer, framework, type, closing, submission method, briefing, validity, estimated value, CIDB requirement, contact.

### Stage 2 — Eligibility determination (15 min)
Use `templates/eligibility-determination.md`. Verdict with sources. **Stop here if NO-GO.**

### Stage 3 — Requirement extraction (45 min)
Read the whole pack. Produce the five-layer skeleton map. Capture the verbatim evaluation criteria.

### Stage 4 — Compliance matrix (60 min)
Every requirement, one row, clause reference, owner, D-minus due date. Use `templates/compliance-matrix.csv`.

### Stage 5 — Go/no-go (20 min)
`templates/go-no-go-checklist.md`. Output BID / NO-BID / BID IF.

### Stage 6 — Client question list (20 min)
Two lists: (a) questions for the **buyer** before the clarification deadline; (b) information requests for the **client**, each with a deadline.

### Stage 7 — Evidence assembly (variable)
Collect, verify, date-check and file every returnable. Update the matrix.

### Stage 8 — Drafting (variable)
Points map → section by section → paragraph rule → self-score.

### Stage 9 — Pricing coordination (variable)
Contractor/QS prices. You run the BOQ audit only. Zero rate suggestions.

### Stage 10 — Red-team (90 min, different day)
`templates/red-team-checklist.md`. Classify defects fatal / scoring / cosmetic. Fix, then re-run Stage 1 kill checks.

### Stage 11 — Packaging & submission (60 min)
`templates/submission-checklist.md`. Index, pagination, labelling, copies, envelopes/upload, proof of submission. Re-check the portal for corrigenda one final time.

### Stage 12 — Debrief (30 min)
What took longest? What did the red team find? What goes into the Tender Bible and the error log?

## 5. Capstone timed test (Day 6)

Take a **fourth**, completely unseen pack. Set a 90-minute timer. Answer all thirteen graduation questions in writing, each with a clause reference:

1. What is being procured?
2. Who qualifies?
3. Does this client qualify?
4. What will automatically disqualify them?
5. What is scored?
6. How is it scored?
7. Which documents are required?
8. Which technical responses must be written?
9. What questions must we ask the client?
10. What must the contractor price themselves?
11. What must be completed/signed?
12. What is the submission method and deadline?
13. What risks could make bidding pointless?

Then open `worklog/baseline-day0.md` and read what you wrote on Day 0. That comparison is the point of the whole programme.

## 7. Readiness gate before your first paid client

Do not take responsibility for a live submission until **all** of these are true:

- [ ] **20+ tender packs analysed**, including **10+ construction** packs
- [ ] You can produce an accurate **eligibility check** independently
- [ ] You can produce an accurate **compliance matrix** independently
- [ ] You have hit the 90-minute standard on an unseen pack
- [ ] Your red-team process has caught planted defects reliably
- [ ] You can state your scope boundary out loud without hesitating
- [ ] Your Tender Bible is populated and in daily use

**First client profile:** an experienced **2GB / 3GB / 3CE**-type contractor bidding straightforward work. Learn fast while limiting the consequences of beginner errors. Do not make a Grade 8 civils pack your first engagement.

**Selling timeline:** the first 14 days are learning only. Prospect research may start earlier; live submission responsibility does not.

## 8. Day 91 onwards — the compounding routine

| Frequency | Action |
|---|---|
| Daily | Analyse one pack; add one Tender Bible entry |
| Weekly | Check Public Procurement Act commencement status and Treasury regulation developments; review awards data on eTenders for pricing and competitor intelligence |
| Monthly | Re-verify CIDB value limits, form versions and your template library |
| Per bid | Debrief; request buyer debriefing where available; log the outcome and the reason |
| Quarterly | Re-read one official guide in full |

Track your own hit rate by tender type. After 20 bids you will know which work you should be chasing — and that is when this becomes a business rather than a skill.
